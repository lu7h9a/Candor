SysScope is installed but not running (it is never enabled by default).

SysScope's daemon watches the processes of EVERY user on this computer:
the programs they start, files they open and connections they make.

  Start it now and at every boot:   sudo systemctl enable --now sysscoped
  Let your user use the GUI/CLI:    sudo usermod -aG sysscope "$USER"
  Also allow follow mode and act:   sudo usermod -aG sysscope-act "$USER"
  (log out and back in after usermod)

Then run `sysscope-gui` or `sysscope status`.
Documentation: /usr/share/doc/sysscope/README.md
